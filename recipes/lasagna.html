Lasagna

Description

The Spicy Vegetarian Lasagna is a vibrant and flavorful dish that brings together a medley of colorful bell peppers—red, orange, yellow, and green—sautéed with onions to create a hearty vegetable base. These vegetables are simmered in a rich tomato sauce infused with a hint of heat from crushed red pepper flakes, offering a delightful kick to the palate.

Layered between tender lasagna noodles, this zesty vegetable mixture is complemented by generous amounts of melted cheese, creating a harmonious blend of textures and flavors. The result is a comforting and satisfying meal that appeals to both vegetarians and spice enthusiasts alike.

This lasagna not only serves as a delicious main course but also makes for excellent leftovers, as the flavors continue to meld and deepen over time. It is an ideal choice for those looking to enjoy a meatless meal without compromising on taste or heartiness.

Ingredients

  • 1 (16 ounce) package lasagna noodles
  • 2 teaspoons olive oil
  • ⅔ cup diced red bell pepper
  • ⅔ cup diced orange bell pepper
  • ⅔ cup diced yellow bell pepper
  • ⅔ cup diced green bell pepper
  • 1 small yellow onion, diced
  • 2 (14.5 ounce) cans diced tomatoes
  • 1 (6 ounce) can tomato paste
  • 1 ½ cups water
  • 1 dash crushed red pepper flakes
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese (Optional)
  • 1 (15 ounce) container ricotta cheese
  • 1 (8 ounce) package shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 4 eggs
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • ¼ teaspoon dried oregano, crushed
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese (Optional)
  1. Step 1: Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook lasagna pasta in boiling water for 8 to 10 minutes, or until al dente. Drain, rinse with cold water, and place on wax paper to cool.
  2. Step 2: Cook bell peppers and onion in olive oil in a large saucepan until onions are translucent. Stir in diced tomatoes, tomato paste, water, and red pepper flakes. More red pepper flakes can be added if spicier sauce is preferred. Simmer for 30 minutes.
  3. Step 3: Preheat oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C). In a medium bowl, combine Parmesan cheese, ricotta cheese, mozzarella cheese, eggs, black pepper, and oregano.
  4. Step 4: Place a small amount of sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish. Reserve 1/2 cup of the sauce. Place three lasagna noodles lengthwise in the pan. Layer some of the cheese mixture and the vegetable sauce on top of the noodles. Repeat layering with remaining ingredients, ending with noodles. Spread reserved sauce over the top of the noodles. Sprinkle with grated Parmesan cheese, if desired.
  5. Step 5: Cover dish with foil, and bake for 40 minutes or until bubbly. Remove foil during the last 10 minutes of baking.
