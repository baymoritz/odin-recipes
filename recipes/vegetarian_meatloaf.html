Vegetarian Meatloaf

Description

For those vegetarians who miss the taste of meatloaf, here is a tasty vegetarian version that matches the flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 (12 ounce) bottle BBQ sauce, or to taste
  • 1 (12 ounce) package vegetarian burger crumbles
  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • ⅓ cup minced onion
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • ½ cup soft bread crumbs
  • 3 tablespoons vegetarian Parmesan cheese
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • ¼ teaspoon dried thyme
  • ¼ teaspoon dried basil
  • ¼ teaspoon parsley flakes
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  1. Preheat the oven to 325 degrees F (165 degrees C). Lightly grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
  2. Combine 1/2 of the BBQ sauce, vegetarian burger crumbles, green bell pepper, onion, garlic, bread crumbs, Parmesan cheese, and beaten egg in a large bowl.
  3. Season with thyme, basil, parsley, salt, and pepper.
  4. Transfer mixture into the prepared loaf pan.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven for 45 minutes.
  6. Pour remaining BBQ sauce over the loaf, and continue baking until loaf is set, about 15 minutes more.
